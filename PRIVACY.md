# NEON SHIFT – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

NEON SHIFT is an offline puzzle game.

- **No personal data is collected.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- **No internet connection is needed to play.** The game does not send any data to us or to third parties.
- **What is stored on your device:** your level progress, stars, coins, hints, daily-challenge streak and settings (music, sound, vibration). This stays on your phone in the app's local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the game.
- **Permissions:** the Android build may request the vibration permission, used only for short haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in Settings.
- **Children:** the game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

If this changes (for example, if ads or purchases are added in a future version), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
