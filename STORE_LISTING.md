# NEON SHIFT – Google Play listing draft

**App name:** NEON SHIFT
**Short description (80 chars max):** One swipe. Two worlds. Opposite moves. A neon logic puzzle.

**Full description:**

Guide two linked characters through two neon worlds at the same time.

Every swipe moves **Light** in the direction you swipe and **Shadow** the opposite way. If either one would hit a wall, neither moves. Find the one route that works on both boards and bring both characters to their exits.

- 20 hand-checked puzzles across two worlds, every one proven solvable
- A new Daily Challenge every day, the same puzzle for every player
- Earn up to 3 stars per level by solving in the fewest moves
- Undo, restart and hints when you get stuck
- Achievements, player level and daily streaks
- Calm synth music, sound effects and haptics
- Plays fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking

**Category:** Puzzle
**Tags:** logic, brain, minimal, neon, offline
**Content rating:** Everyone (no violence, no user content, no data collection)
**Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/mianalihassan0038-ui/NeonShift/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
